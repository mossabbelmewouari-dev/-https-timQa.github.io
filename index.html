Since 1987 · Marrakech

Three generations.
One obsession.

Our grandfather built the first stone press in 1987, guided by the belief that the Maghreb's soil held secrets no industrial process could replicate. He passed this obsession to our father, who refined the cold-press method, and then to us.

Today, TIMⵇA (meaning "The Pure" in Tamazight, the Amazigh language) carries that legacy — uncompromised, unadulterated, and deeply rooted in Moroccan culture.
